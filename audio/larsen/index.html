Feedback: each line saturates inside its own loop — push FEEDBACK past 1.00 (red thumb) to self-oscillate with no input. N sets active lines per bank; SER chains, PAR sums.
Ping-pong: the echo bank feeds a cross-coupled delay whose repeats bounce L↔R, decaying by Bounce Feedback each hop. Send crossfades the echo from centered to bouncing; Damp darkens the repeats. The loop drone stays centered.
Stereo center: Width scales the side signal (the bouncing echoes) — 0 folds to mono. Center scales the mid (the loop drone). Balance is L/R. Bitstream corrupts the final image; SPLIT decorrelates L/R for extra width.
