Space plays. DCW is the Casio CZ name for how hard the phase is bent: 0% is a pure cosine, 100% is the sharpest shape the pitch allows without aliasing. Seed sets every random choice (noise, partial detune and pan), so the same patch and seed always render the same sound; the entropy noise type is the exception and is fresh each render. WAVs are 24-bit stereo at the playback sample rate and arrive zipped.
