technicolor interposition

A loaded video streams constantly underneath — picture and sound both — at a stable depth. Only the synth bed takes the hard occlusion when the two streams collide; the video keeps running through every event. At the collision a frozen copy of the frame tears into bands and hard-cuts in front, blooming additively in the video's own color, while a saturated swell of the video's own audio punches up over its constant layer. Then it lifts, and the video is revealed having moved on. Found, not arranged.

Constant layer: once started, the video's own audio plays continuously through the synth bed (set with video level); the interposition adds a bloomed, saturated swell of it rather than replacing it — image and sound stay running underneath, only the synth bed is cut for the contour click.
