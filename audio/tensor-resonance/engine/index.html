TENSOR SLICES 5×5×5 — T[color][loudness][frequency] · click +kick · shift-click −kick

Each slice is one COLOR (RED · PURPLE · BLUE · GREEN · YELLOW). Rows = LOUD · LOUD~SOFT · SOFT · SOFT~MID · MIDDLE, columns = frequency bands F₁ · F₁·₂ · F₂ · F₂·₃ · F₃. The in-between slots (purple, green, and the two in-between rows and columns) are variable blends of their neighbours: set them on the left, or let them follow the tensor. Tucker/HOSVD splits the tensor into 3 modes × 5 singular values = 15 σ. Each σ owns one triad of three unaligned microtonal frequencies. In the classic flow, timbre comes from the COLOR factor, envelope from the LOUDNESS factor, drift scale from the FREQUENCY factor. In the v2 flow, each triad's query attends over all 125 cells, and they come from where its attention lands.

FLOW READOUT — relaxation clock · cross-attention between modes · where each triad listens · aspects

Top left: the six cross-attention maps (rows = the querying mode's slices, columns = the other mode's slices). Right: each triad's attention over the 125 cells, grouped by COLOR slice (no fixed alignment); its source is its own σ, another σ (←) or a free query (∗), redrawn every epoch. Bottom: brightness opens the high end (and sends CC74), fluency scales the drift, prosody scales how often triads re-roll; w is each σ's equation of state, and a triad re-rolls when w rises through −1 (phantom crossing, flashes white).

MPE gives every frequency its own channel and pitch bend, so nothing is rounded. Use it with MPE synths (Surge XT, Vital, Pianoteq), set to MPE with the same bend range. Single channel rounds to the nearest semitone on one channel. Use it for Dexed or any non-MPE synth; DEXED PRESET sets channel 1 and 3 triads. TEST NOTE plays a chord on the chosen port and channel, so you can hear whether the synth is listening. Dexed: a MIDI Program Change can crash Dexed 1.0.x (a bug in Dexed). To change its sound, DEXED VOICE and the epoch option send a whole voice as SysEx instead, which is safe; the voices come from your Dexed cartridges through the Python engine. Give this its own lane: add an IAC bus named Tensor (Audio MIDI Setup → IAC Driver → Ports → +) and pick it here and in the synth, so it never mixes with other streams on IAC Bus 1. If the Python server is sending MIDI (./run.sh --midi), leave this off.
