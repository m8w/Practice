fractal timing — the drums are a recursive subdivision tree: each span of time may split into 2–3 unequal parts, the same rule applied at every scale (a Cantor-style point field). Shallow nodes sound low & sparse, deep nodes high & quiet. No global grid, so there's pulse-feel without a downbeat.
beatlessness bends the split ratios from even (metric, pulse-like) toward the golden ratio (aperiodic). melody grouping expands one contour: every note becomes a transposed, time-scaled copy of the whole motif. Each span is generated fresh — release-as-found. Record captures the output to .webm.
