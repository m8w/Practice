Distances between simultaneous keys
Abstract random rhythm

When a section turns abstract, one or more instrument families leave the written rhythm while the others keep it.

Kinds of rhythm
Tuplet grid

Each bar is redivided into 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 or 13 equal parts and every note moves to the nearest one, so families run in different tuplets against each other.

Stochastic time

The notes of each bar keep their order, but the gaps between them are drawn at random, so pulses stretch and bunch.

Aleatoric window

Within two-bar windows, notes enter at free moments with free lengths and some drop out, like a Lutosławski ad libitum passage.

Polytempo

A family plays its material at another speed (2:3, 3:4, 4:5, 5:4, 4:3, 5:7), looping until the section ends, in the manner of Ives.
