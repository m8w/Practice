Endless Station

Three endless, generative pages built from public-domain music. Everything they need is in this folder.

Aggregate FieldModern methods after Cage, Feldman, Babbitt, Eno, Roach, Glass, Riley, Adams, Pärt, Reich, Fripp and the public-domain modernists, layered on their own clocks, with a counting chorus. Endless SymphonyNew symphonies planned after the public-domain catalog, with polytonality, microtones and abstract rhythm. Endless SonataNew piano pieces grown from 400 works and your Beethoven favorites, on piano, harpsichord, organ and more.

Opened from disk, the pages play with every instrument. Run Endless Station.command to add spoken announcements in Dispatch and a now-playing file for OBS.
