Touch or click the surface to bow the string. Hold several fingers to play chords. Turn the radius while you hold.

R and 1/R give the same partials with the stereo sides swapped. Large or small R gives a harmonic ladder.
Tower partials sit at pitch × np. 0.5 is a string, 1.0 is a harmonic series.
Higher T lets more of the tower sound. Near the top you hit the Hagedorn wall.
Regge tower (hollow ticks) against the mass-formula partials.

Six vacua

Each bar is the chance of finding that radius when you measure. The amplitudes drift, and a measurement collapses them to one radius by the Born rule. Three of the six are T-dual pairs (0.50/2.00, 0.80/1.25, 0.28/3.60).

Partials

momentum n winding w oscillator N mixed Regge tower

Mass formula: M² = (n/R)² + (wR)² + 2(N + Ñ), with N − Ñ = nw. Partial frequency = fundamental × M / Mmin. Momentum modes pan left, winding modes pan right.

Keys: Z–/, A–;, Q–P play notes upward through the scale. [ ] turn the radius. Space measures.
